Delete your K&U account

K&U is published on Google Play and the App Store by Team M. One account works in the app and on the K&U campus websites, so deleting it here removes it everywhere.

Or ask us by email

Email TeamMillionM@gmail.com with the subject “K&U account deletion request”, from the email address of your account (for Apple sign-in, the address Apple shared with us, which may be a relay address). Never send your password. We may reply to confirm the request is yours, and we delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, so the account can no longer be used.
  • Your profile on every campus: name, country, length of stay, university.
  • Your posts with their replies and likes, your replies to others, and your likes, on every campus board and the board all campuses share.
  • Photos you uploaded with posts.
  • Your live chat messages and presence in every room.
  • Your saved class week and classes you added by hand.
  • Your notification settings, notification history and device tokens, blocks and hidden posts.

What is kept

  • Reports you filed stay with the moderators so they can finish them, without your account identifier. The report's reason and any note you wrote stay with it.
  • Reports other students filed about your posts or messages, which can name your account identifier and display name, stay with the moderators' decisions so they can finish them and keep the boards safe.
  • Email you sent us, including a deletion request, stays in our inbox.
  • A photo posted on the website on 1 or 2 August 2026 was stored without an account attached, so deletion cannot find it. If one of those was yours, email us and we remove it.

Deletion takes effect at once. Nothing deleted is kept in a separate copy by us; Google, which hosts our database, may hold it in its own short-lived system backups before they expire.
